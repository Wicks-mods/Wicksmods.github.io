Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

Wick runs two small posting tools on Meta's platform. Both are personal: the only person who uses them is Wick, who owns the Facebook Pages they post to. This page covers those tools and wicksmods.com itself.

What the tools do

They publish posts to two Facebook Pages that Wick owns. Wick's Mods gets addon release notes and news. Wickidspliff gets an announcement when a stream goes live on Twitch or YouTube.

What they can access

Only those two Pages, through access the Page owner granted. They use it to publish posts and to check that a post went through.

What they collect

Nothing about anyone else. They do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about people who view, follow or interact with either Page, and they never ask anyone but the Page owner to log in.

Where data is kept

The access tokens that let the tools post are kept only on the Page owner's own computer. Nothing else is stored.

Deleting data

Because the tools keep no data about other people, there is nothing to delete. The Page owner can remove their access at any time in Facebook, under Settings, then Business Integrations.

This website

wicksmods.com has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is served by Cloudflare. Both see the usual technical details of a web request, such as your IP address.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the Wick's Mods Facebook Page, or ask in the Wick's Mods Discord.
