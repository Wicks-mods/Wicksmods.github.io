Wickidspliff Uploader

A personal upload tool for the Wickidspliff YouTube channel

Wickidspliff Uploader is a small program that runs on Wickidspliff's own computer. It uploads Wickidspliff's own gaming videos, such as stream recordings and highlights, to the Wickidspliff YouTube channel.

Built on YouTube API Services

The uploader uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) to upload videos and to confirm it is signed in to the right channel. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Wickidspliff channel. It is not a public service, there is nothing to sign up for, and it never asks anyone else to sign in with Google.

What it does

  • Signs in once with Google, with permission to upload videos and to see the channel's basic details.
  • Uploads a video file chosen by the channel owner, with the title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy setting and schedule the owner sets.
  • Checks that it is uploading to the Wickidspliff channel and no other.

It doesn't read, change or delete any other videos, comments, subscribers or analytics.

Privacy and terms

What the uploader stores, and how to revoke its access, is in the Privacy Policy. The rules for using it are in the Terms of Service.
