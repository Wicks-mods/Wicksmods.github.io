Privacy Policy

Wickidspliff Uploader · last updated October 7, 2026

Wickidspliff Uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos to the Wickidspliff YouTube channel. Only the owner of that channel uses it. This policy explains what it accesses, what it keeps, and how to remove its access.

YouTube API Services

Wickidspliff Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own collection and use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the uploader asks for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload), to upload the videos and thumbnails the owner chooses.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly), only to read the signed-in channel's name and ID, so it can confirm it is uploading to the Wickidspliff channel.

It does not access anyone else's account, and it never reads viewers' information, comments, subscriber lists or analytics.

What it stores, and where

  • Sign-in credentials. The Google sign-in token is saved in a file on the channel owner's own computer, so the owner doesn't have to sign in for every upload. It is not sent anywhere except to Google.
  • An upload log. A local log notes each upload's file name, title and video link. Entries older than 30 days are deleted automatically.

Nothing is stored on a server, and nothing is stored about anyone other than the channel owner.

How it uses and shares data

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or combined with other data. Videos and their details go only to YouTube.

Cookies and tracking

The uploader is a desktop program and sets no cookies. These web pages have no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the pages are served by Cloudflare; both see the usual technical details of a web request, such as your IP address.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove Wickidspliff Uploader's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps and services). Once access is removed, the saved token stops working.

To delete everything it keeps, delete the saved token and the upload log on the computer where it runs. To ask for deletion, or with any question about this policy, contact Wickidspliff through the Wickidspliff Facebook Page. Requests are handled within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
